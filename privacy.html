Privacy Policy

Spenderrr · Last updated 27 October 2026

Spenderrr is a free expense tracker that runs in your web browser at spend-log-v1.netlify.app. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains what happens to your data.

Your expenses stay on your device

Everything you enter (amounts, categories, dates, notes and bill splits) is saved only in your browser's storage on your own phone or computer. It is never sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer. The developer cannot see your entries.

Backups

You choose where backups go:

  • A folder on your device, which you pick. The app writes one backup file there.
  • Files app or Downloads, when you tap to save a backup.
  • Your own Google Drive, only if you tap "Back up to Google Drive" and sign in.

Google Drive and Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for the drive.file permission only. This lets the app create and update its own backup files in your Drive (the folder spend-expense-backup) and read them back when you tap Restore. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.

  • The backup goes directly from your browser to your Google Drive. It does not pass through any server run by the developer.
  • To show which account your backups go to, the app reads your Google account name and email address (through the same drive.file permission). They are saved only on your device, are not included in backups, and are removed when you disconnect Google Drive.
  • The developer never receives your Google account details, your sign-in token or your backup files.
  • Google user data is used only to save and restore your own backup. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app (Backup → Disconnect Google Drive) or from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Spenderrr's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies, analytics and other services

The app does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. Its fonts are stored with the app, so no font service is contacted. The website is hosted by Netlify, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as described in Netlify's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

To delete your data, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app from your home screen. Delete any backup files from your device folder or Google Drive yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
