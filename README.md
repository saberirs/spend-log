# spend-log
A free, no-login expense tracker for your phone, with colored categories, weekly and monthly totals, Excel reports, and Splitwise-style bill splitting.


Daily Spend Log

A simple expense tracker that runs on any iPhone or Android phone. No account, no sign-in, and no ads. Install it from the link and it works like a regular app, even offline.

Track spending quickly. Type the amount and tap a category. The date and time are filled in for you. Food is split into Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks, alongside Grocery, Shopping, Household, Utilities, Transport, and Other, each with its own color.

See where your money goes. Weekly and monthly views show your total, your daily average, a comparison with the previous period, a day-by-day chart, and a breakdown by category.

Split bills with friends. Split any expense equally, by exact amounts, or by percentage, and choose who paid. The Split tab shows who owes you and whom you owe. Record payments when someone settles up, or send them a ready-made summary.

Check payment messages. Paste a bank or card text. The app picks out the amount and merchant, suggests a category, and asks whether to add it.

Download Excel reports. Export any week, month, or your full history as a workbook with sheets for every expense, category totals, daily and weekly totals, and splits.

Private by design. Your data stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere. Save a backup file anytime and restore it on a new phone.
